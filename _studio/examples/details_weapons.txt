Ancient soldiers feared ONE weapon more than any sword… and to this day, nobody knows how it was made. 🔥

Stix tested 10 ancient weapons and ranked them from weakest to deadliest:
#10 Club: literally a stick. Bonk.
#9 Dagger: fast and sneaky, but you have to get WAY too close.
#8 Sling: a rock and a strap that hits like a bullet.
#7 Battle Axe: built for one job, smashing shields.
#6 Mace: armor doesn't matter.
#5 Khopesh: Egypt's hooked sword that ripped shields out of enemy hands.
#4 Spear: cheap, long reach, the true king of ancient battlefields.
#3 Composite Bow: horse archers could hit targets from about 200 meters.
#2 Ballista: a giant crossbow that punched through shield walls.
#1 Greek Fire: the Byzantine liquid flame that kept burning on water. Its exact recipe is still lost.

Which one would YOU pick? Tell me in the comments 👇
