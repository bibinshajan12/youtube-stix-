# Republic Minutes — Trump: Decisions, Mercy, and the Human Record

This folder contains a 20-episode YouTube Shorts package about documented constructive decisions, acts of clemency, humanitarian measures, and bipartisan laws signed by President Donald Trump.

## Editorial promise

The series is favorable but evidence-led. It does not declare that a public figure is “good” and ask viewers to accept it. Each episode shows a documented choice, the person or group affected, the result, and one fair limitation or shared-credit note. The audience is allowed to reach the character judgment.

## Package

- [20 complete scripts with archival-image plans and primary sources](./20-archival-image-shorts.md)
- Each spoken script targets approximately 50 seconds.
- Every episode includes a title, thumbnail phrase, first-frame hook, real-image edit plan, and primary-source links.
- Image sourcing prioritizes U.S. government material and files whose individual Wikimedia Commons pages identify a reusable license.
